# Context / Token Optimization Workflow

1. Measure current cost/latency/context load where possible; if not, establish a qualitative baseline and label it.
2. Map authoritative files and duplicated content. Identify high-token, low-value, stale, conflicting and frequently loaded material.
3. Protect critical requirements, decisions, safety, acceptance, evidence, checkpoint and source-of-truth pointers.
4. Propose pruning, deduplication, progressive disclosure, concise routing, selective retrieval or reference links.
5. Do not delete/merge authoritative information without review; do not optimize away constraints or traceability.
6. Make one bounded change, compare before/after and verify links/invocation behavior.
7. Report measured vs estimated savings, quality trade-offs, risks and rollback.
